Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Dino T-Rex Runner experience.

How do I play?

Use the up arrow key to jump over obstacles and the down arrow key to duck. On mobile, tap the screen to jump.

How is the score calculated?

Your score increases the further you run without hitting an obstacle. Speed increases as you progress!

Is there a mobile version?

Yes, the game is fully responsive and can be played directly in any mobile browser without downloading an app.

Do I need an account to save my score?

Currently, high scores are saved locally on your device and can be submitted to our global leaderboard after a run.

Is the game free to play?

Yes, Dino T-Rex Runner is completely free to play with no hidden costs or in-app purchases.
